## 2026-03-05 - Context-Specific Modal Close ARIA Labels

**Learning:** Generic or non-descriptive ARIA labels on modal close buttons (such as `aria-label="Close modal"` or `aria-label="Close image modal"`) cause confusion for screen reader users when navigating pages with multiple distinct modals (e.g., booking, privacy policy, cookies policy, food image, menu modals).
**Action:** Ensure every modal close button includes a specific, contextual ARIA label clearly identifying which modal context is being closed.
