Useful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding location, reservations, beer garden seating and B&B room stays at The White Horse Inn.

Where is The White Horse Inn located in Cromer?

We are situated in central Cromer at 24 West Street, Cromer, Norfolk, NR27 9DS. We are located just a short walk from Cromer Pier, the beach front, and local town shops.

Does The White Horse Inn Cromer have a beer garden?

Yes! We feature a spacious, dog-friendly outdoor beer garden with wooden bench seating. Outdoor tables operate on a first-come, walk-in basis with full bar service available.

Do you serve traditional Sunday roasts?

Yes, we serve traditional Norfolk Sunday roasts with giant Yorkshire puddings, roast potatoes and rich gravy all day every Sunday. Online table booking is recommended.

How do I reserve a table or enquire about B&B rooms?

You can book a dining table instantly using the "Make a Table Reservation" button on our site. For B&B room stays, click "Enquire About Rooms" or contact us directly on 01263 512272.
