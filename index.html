Heritage & Hospitality

A Classic Coastal Pub on West Street

Situated at 24 West Street in Cromer, The White Horse Inn is a traditional coastal pub offering warm Norfolk hospitality to locals and seaside visitors alike.

Whether you are stopping by for a cold pint after a walk on Cromer beach, catching live sports on our HD screens, enjoying a plate of hearty pub food, or staying overnight in our en-suite rooms, we look forward to giving you a warm welcome.
