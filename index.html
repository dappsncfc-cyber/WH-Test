Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding location, reservations, beer garden seating and B&B room stays at The White Horse Inn.

Where is The White Horse Inn located in Cromer?

We are situated in central Cromer at 24 West Street, Cromer, Norfolk, NR27 9DS. We are located just a short walk from Cromer Pier, the beach front, and local town shops.

Does The White Horse Inn Cromer have a beer garden?

Yes! We feature a spacious, dog-friendly outdoor beer garden with wooden bench seating. Outdoor tables operate on a first-come, walk-in basis with full bar service available.

Do you serve traditional Sunday roasts?

Yes, we serve traditional Norfolk Sunday roasts with giant Yorkshire puddings, roast potatoes and rich gravy all day every Sunday. Online table booking is recommended.

How do I reserve a table or enquire about B&B rooms?

You can book a dining table instantly using the "Make a Table Reservation" button on our site. For B&B room stays, click "Enquire About Rooms" or contact us directly on 01263 512275.

Cookies Policy

The White Horse Inn Cookies Policy

UK PECR & GDPR Compliant • Last Updated: January 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, mobile device, or tablet when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, store user preferences, provide secure browsing, and deliver reporting information to website operators.

2. UK Legislation & Compliance

Our use of cookies complies with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and the UK GDPR. Under these laws, strictly necessary cookies do not require prior user consent, whereas functional, performance, and third-party cookies require transparent disclosure.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

A. Essential & Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function correctly. They enable core functionalities such as session navigation, secure table booking widget rendering (TableAgent), and B&B room enquiry form submissions (FreeToBook).

B. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous statistical information (e.g. pages visited, load times). This enables us to continuously improve site layout and user experience.

C. Third-Party Embedded Content Cookies

Our site embeds trusted external services, such as Google Maps for location directions and reservation widgets. These third-party services may place cookies on your device according to their respective privacy and cookie policies.

4. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You can control and manage cookie settings directly in your web browser. Most browsers allow you to block, delete, or receive notifications before a cookie is stored. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact the functionality of online reservation widgets.

For detailed instructions on managing cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding our Cookies Policy or how we handle your browsing data, please contact us at info@whitehorsecromer.co.uk or call 01263 512275.
