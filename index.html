Authentic Coastal Pub

A Warm Welcome on West Street

Whether you are winding down after walking the coastal path, meeting friends to watch live football on our big screens, or enjoying a delicious Sunday roast, White Horse Inn offers authentic coastal pub hospitality.

Situated at 24 West Street in Cromer, our inn combines traditional pub character with bright, comfortable modern spaces—including our main bar lounge, dining areas, and enclosed rear garden patio.
